1,400+ Grade-5 Titanium Fasteners

Every single bolt, nut, and washer across the chassis is individually CNC-milled from titanium alloy, mirror-polished by hand, and laser-etched with the Araldi heraldic crest. No standard catalogue fasteners exist within an Araldi.

Carbo-Titanium HP62 Weave

By interweaving continuous Grade-5 titanium filaments directly into aeronautical carbon fibers, the monocoque achieves unprecedented impact toughness and a torsional rigidity of 58,000 Nm/degree without shattering.

Swiss Horological Cockpit

We reject planned obsolescence. Zero capacitive screens or plastic touchpads. The instrument binnacles are genuine Swiss-crafted mechanical chronometers with sapphire crystal faces and illuminated mechanical ruby bearings.

Symmetrical Carbon Spine

The carbon-fiber weave along the vehicle’s central centerline aligns with a tolerance of 0.5 millimeters from the nose splitter to the rear diffuser, creating an unbroken chevron tapestry of composite craftsmanship.
